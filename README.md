## 👋 About Me
If I'm not lifting or playing basketball, I'm most likely coding.
I graduated from Toronto Metropolitan University with a degree in Computer Science and I'm currently a Data Engineer at SecondaryLink, building ingestion pipelines, ETL and data quality systems for fund performance data.
I love building things that serve a purpose and make people's lives easier, from data pipelines and RAG systems to full-stack web apps.
Most importantly, I am always learning!
